Compost Tiers & Reward Pools
How the ring value system works

Each floor (2–4) has 3 concentric rings of farm plots. When the total sell value of crops planted in a ring meets its threshold, that ring activates and adds a permanent income % bonus. Bonuses are additive and stack across all active rings on all floors.

Floors 3 and 4 also have Soil Quality upgrades (up to 15 levels), each adding +3.57% income.
